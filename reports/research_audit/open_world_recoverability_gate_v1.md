## 1. Question

Does selective runtime Evidence acquisition reduce avoidable false-Unknown
decisions on Evidence-recoverable Known observations WITHOUT materially
degrading True Unknown recognition?

**Answer: NO.** Under the frozen RF-only protocol with policy-conditioned
MSP novelty scoring, the typed utility policy makes recoverable-Known
false-Unknown rejection **materially worse** (FURK +0.235 mean vs direct,
worse in 9/9 cells, bootstrap CI [0.08, 0.29]) while True Unknown
recognition is preserved (AUROC/recall unchanged or slightly better) and
Known classification improves (Macro-F1 +0.015 mean). Classification
recovery does NOT transfer to novelty-score recovery.

## 5. Mechanism (evidence, not speculation)

1. Acquisition under the utility policy concentrates on rows where the
   Basic model is wrong or uncertain — exactly the recoverable-Known rows.
   Post-acquisition MSP novelty scores (1 − max proba of the acquired
   evidence model) on those rows remain high: the evidence models are also
   uncertain on the hard rows, so acquired rows cross the policy-calibrated
   threshold more often than under P0 → FURK rises.
2. The P7 oracle result shows this is NOT a routing-quality problem:
   even analysis-only perfect routing (P7, never deployable) leaves
   residual Known FUR ≈ P0 (0.140 vs 0.143 on seed 20260817/Credential) and
   total Known rejection only 0.039 → 0.053. The bottleneck is the novelty
   SCORING under evidence models, not which rows acquire.
3. Classification recovery transfers (Macro-F1, recovery rate), novelty
   recovery does not: under MSP, an acquired Evidence model that improves
   the hard row's classification does not improve its max-proba confidence
   enough to avoid crossing the fixed-FPR threshold.
4. Utility vs difficulty (mandatory B14 comparison): P6 does NOT beat
   generic difficulty routing on FURK — vs LOW_CONFIDENCE: +0.052/+0.074/
   +0.002; vs HIGH_ENTROPY: +0.344/+0.075/−0.139 (worse in 2/3 rotations).
   Per the pre-registered rule: utility-specific routing value is NOT
   established for the false-Unknown objective. (P6 does beat both on Known
   Macro-F1, but the FURK comparison governs this claim.)
5. Specialization audit (non-gating): evidence utility is strongly
   class-specific — Benign has large positive net utility (T +0.097, R
   +0.216, TR +0.159) while Recon_Scanning has strongly negative (T −0.112,
   R −0.488, TR −0.266) in the Credential rotation. Statistics only; never
   converted into runtime rules.

  action is `REASSESS_RECOVERABILITY_CONDITIONED_OPEN_WORLD_MAINLINE`.
  The recoverability-conditioned open-world mainline as specified
  (MSP novelty + typed utility acquisition + fixed-FPR calibration) is NOT
  supported by this gate. The result does NOT invalidate Gate 1B's
  classification-utility finding (Macro-F1/recovery gains replicate), nor
  the C1 distinction (INSUFFICIENT_OBSERVATION_OF_KNOWN != TRUE_NOVELTY)

  post-acquisition MSP novelty scoring does not transfer classification
  recovery to novelty recovery. Any reassessment must address novelty
  scoring under acquired evidence (e.g., evidence-conditioned novelty
  calibration) BEFORE re-testing acquisition value for open-world routing.
